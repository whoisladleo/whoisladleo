## 👋 About me

I'm a technical executive based in Tanzania. As CTO of **Tunzaa**, I lead the architecture and engineering of a multi-tenant marketplace and payment platform, including white-label APIs that let partners launch on our rails and agent-ready APIs that let AI agents initiate payments. I care about systems that hold up in real conditions: mobile-money integrations, unreliable networks, many tenants on one platform, and money that has to reconcile.

## 🚀 Focus: Tunzaa

- Multi-tenant marketplace and payment infrastructure
- White-label platform and partner-integrated delivery flows
- Agent-ready APIs and agent-initiated payments, so AI agents can transact alongside people and businesses
- Reliability, observability and codebase quality across the platform
- Building the engineering team and processes so the platform scales beyond any one person
